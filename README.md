In 5G-enabled Cognitive Radio Vehicular Ad-hoc Networks (CR-VANETs), static spectrum allocation and conventional machine-learning approaches—including heuristic rule- based systems, SVM/KNN classifiers, and Seg-CR-VANETs- are limited by high mobility, rapidly changing topologies, high false-alarm rates, and adversarial attacks. To address these issues, we propose TCAS-HGNN-FL, a Traffic Collision Avoidance System modeling a Hypergraph Neural Network and Federated Learning. Hypergraph modeling captures multi-vehicle interactions beyond pairwise graphs, while privacy-preserving federated learning enables distributed model aggregation, proactive spectrum sensing, and collision mitigation. Compared with traditional methods, standard ML baselines, and routing protocols IDRL, ARIOR, I- AREOR, and AREOR, TCAS-HGNN-FL achieves Pd = 1.00, reduces Pfa to 0.01, and lowers Pcoll by 79.16% versus non-ML and 69.77% versus standard ML methods. It increases secondary-user throughput by 38.75%, improves the throughput–sensing-time trade-off by 54.04%, and retains 97.49% detection accuracy under a 50% malicious-node ratio. With collision-aware routing and 80% RSU coverage, it achieves 99.8% PDR, reduces delay by approximately 34%, PLR by 41.83%, and control overhead to 4700 (at 80% RSU coverage) . These results indicate that combining higher-order hypergraph modeling, privacy-preserving federated learning, and traffic-aware collision prediction establishes a secure, scalable, low-latency, and spectrum-efficient solution for 5G and beyond.
